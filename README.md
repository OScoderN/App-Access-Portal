# What are Sessions, Devices, and Apperties?

Top to bottom:

# Sessions

Sessions are the **building blocks** of AAP. 1 session contains atleast ONE device, be it a `Computer`(Laptops are the same thing), `Phone`, or `OLD Nokia Phone`. The computer, phone, and keyboard/mouse pair in your room is the **physical version of a session**.

***

# Device

You should know what a device is ever since you got an iPad. It's this thing with a battery, a (touch)screen and **volume buttons** and other *dexterity-filled features*. Combine them, and it becomes a device that only has access thanks to it's OS, but on AAP becomes **_a BEAST_**!
