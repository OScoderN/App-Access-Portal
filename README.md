# What are Sessions, Devices, and Apperties?

Top to bottom:

# Sessions

Sessions are the **building blocks** of AAP. 1 session contains atleast ONE device, be it a `Computer`(Laptops are the same thing), `Phone`, or `OLD Nokia Phone`. The computer, phone, and keyboard/mouse pair in your room is the **physical version of a session**.

***

# Device

You should know what a device is ever since you got an iPad. It's this thing with a battery, a (touch)screen and **volume buttons** and other *dexterity-filled features*. Combine them, and it becomes a device that only has access thanks to it's OS, but on AAP becomes **_a BEAST_**!

# Apperties

> # WARNING!
> - THIS IS NOT A TYPO! Appertie is THE CORRECT SPELLING, AND Apperties's is also correct.
> - ~~Apperty~~ IS AN INCORRECT FORM OF APPERTIE, SHOWING THAT THE TYPER OF THAT MALFORMED TEXT FAILED TO PRONOUNCE THE ORIGINAL WORD AND IT'S SPELLING. IT'S APPER-TIE(the thing you wear on your shirt/suit to work), NOT ~~APPERTY~~!!!

An AAP App is called an Appertie. You also call a **converted* [`.apk`/`.iap`/`.app`/`.exe]* an Appertie because it has been converted into an Appertie. You can also convert apperties and keep them in your storage or just… *let AAP consume it*. Apperties can be Found on the ~~AAP Market~~ <u>AAP Store</u>.
